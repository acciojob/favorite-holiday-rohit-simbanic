Kyoto, Japan


Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is a city on the island of Honshu. It is famous for its numerous classical Buddhist temples, serene gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden houses.

  1. Walk through the thousands of vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari Taisha.
  2. Marvel at the stunning reflection of Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion.
  3. Take a peaceful stroll through the towering stalks of the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.
  4. Enjoy the panoramic city views from the wooden stage of Kiyomizu-dera temple.
  5. Wander along the cherry-tree-lined Philosopher's Path during spring.
  • Experience a traditional multi-course Kaiseki dinner.
  • Drink ceremonial grade Matcha green tea paired with sweet wagashi.
  • Savor a comforting bowl of Yudofu, a local tofu hot pot specialty.
